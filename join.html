Open pathways

PhD students

Applicants with backgrounds in materials science, mechanical engineering, physics, chemistry, biomedical engineering, or related areas are encouraged to inquire.

Postdoctoral researchers

Researchers with strong experimental, analytical, or device-development experience are welcome to discuss aligned projects and funding routes.

Research students

Master’s students, research assistants, and visiting students may contact us about project-based opportunities and research training.

How to inquire

Introduce your interests clearly

To help us understand your background and fit, please prepare a concise email explaining why our research interests you and how your experience could contribute.

  • Curriculum vitae, including education and research experience
  • A brief statement of research interests and relevant skills
  • Academic transcripts or a concise grade summary
  • Representative publication, thesis, portfolio, or project link if available
  • Your preferred start date and the position type you are seeking

Add Professor Gao’s verified email address on the Contact page before publishing
